The action 'Rename Specific Files'

  • ... does rename files somewhere under this directory
  • ... works with a wildcard to identify the files to rename

The current directory is:

  • ##DIR##

Details

  • You can specify here one * as a wildcard
  • With the To-name-pattern you specify the new name. This To-name-pattern may contain also a *. The * will replaced by the corresponding content of the From-Name-Pattern.
  • Additionally to the from-name-pattern(e:s) you can also specify the usual file-selectors. The file-selectors will be processed at first. The From-name-pattern (e:o)that with the wildcard(e:c) will base its work on the result of the file-selectors.
  • The file-selectors are optional and used seldom
